Salah Fekkak

Instagram LinkedIn Behance Dribbble Portfolio

I fell in love with graphic design as a teenager, teaching myself Photoshop online to create designs for soccer forums and blogs. My design philosophy prioritizes functionality above all. I strive to create simple, practical, and user-friendly designs. Growing up in a coastal city, I embrace the hues of the ocean especially blue, my favorite color. Fluent in four languages, I enjoy collaborating on multicultural projects with clients from diverse backgrounds. I’m also passionate about working with small businesses, understanding their needs, and helping them enhance their visual identity and online presence.
