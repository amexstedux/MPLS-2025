The power of design has influenced me in incredibly positive ways throughout my life. As a designer myself, I aim to inspire that same positive energy in those around me. I am a graphic and web artist that combines simplicity, cuteness, and comfort into one. I enjoy bright, solid colors and clean linework, whether I'm working with traditional or digital media. Constantly creating, I hope to share all my designs and ideas with the world and make everyone's day just a little bit brighter.
