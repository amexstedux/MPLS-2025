Graphic Design

Kendra Sundermeyer

Instagram LinkedIn Behance Dribbble Portfolio

I thrive on creative challenges and especially love branding, signage, and bringing imaginative concepts to life. I have been an artist at heart all my life, with a passion for creating and crafting. My father, a commercial photographer, was a major inspiration and encouraged me to pursue art and design. Throughout my journey, I’ve explored many creative avenues, including graphic design, prop creation, latex mask making, and costuming for my Etsy shop. These experiences taught me self-branding, marketing, logo design, sculpting, painting, and airbrushing. Even in a corporate setting, I found ways to be creative through presentation, SharePoint, and documentation design. With encouragement from family and colleagues, I returned to college to finish my degree.
