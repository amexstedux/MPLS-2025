Graphic Design

Molly Larson-Robideau

Instagram LinkedIn Behance Dribbble Portfolio

My approach to design is very playful, and I feel like a world covered in color, imagery, textures, bold illustrations, and ART is a beautiful world to exist in. My goal as a designer is to create art that contributes to the beauty in the lives of my audience. I hope to make people smile and encourage them to be creative and a little bit silly in their day-to-day lives. Choose the purse that's shaped like a shoe, add a big smiley face to the end of your signature, paint stars on the rocks outside your house. Find joy and beauty in the details.
