ABOUT THE SHOW

There’s no doubt about it: today’s world moves at a dizzying pace. With its constantly shifting trends and technologies, the world of design is certainly no exception. With this portfolio showcase, we’re thrilled to share with you how this year’s 33 graduating designers have overcome challenges both classic and contemporary. Not only is this collection a retrospective of our journey thus far — it's a glimpse of what’s to come as we continue to thrive and innovate as we transition into our roles in the greater design industry. All that said, we believe a picture’s worth a thousand words, so scroll on and have a look at what we’ve been working on!
